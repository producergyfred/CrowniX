      How to Play

      Setup. 8×8 board, only dark squares are used. Each side has 12 men. White starts at the bottom and moves first.

      Men move one square diagonally forward and capture by jumping an adjacent enemy piece diagonally forward. They never move or capture backward.

      Mandatory capture. If any capture exists you must capture. If several sequences exist you must take the one capturing the most pieces.

      Multiple captures. After a jump, the same piece must keep jumping while it can, and may change direction. Captured pieces are removed when the turn ends and cannot be jumped twice.

      Promotion. A man that ends its move on the far row becomes a king.

      Flying kings move any distance along a diagonal, in any direction. A king may capture an enemy piece and land on any empty square beyond it.

      Winning. You win when the opponent has no pieces or no legal move. Draws: 40 consecutive king moves without a capture or man move, or the same position three times.

      Playing. Tap a piece, then a gold-marked square. During a multiple capture tap each landing square in turn. Hint shows a strong move with the reason; using it, or Undo, makes the game unrated. History replays the game move by move.

      Daily Crown. A new challenge every day, the same for everyone, played offline. Complete it to build a streak.

      Rating. An Elo-style number measured only against the computer levels, stored on this device. There is no online ranking.
