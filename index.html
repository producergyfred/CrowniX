      How to Play

      • Move One square, diagonally forward.
      • Capture Jump forward. Forced, longest chain first.
      • Chain Keep jumping with the same piece.
      • King Reach the far row. Kings fly, both ways.
      • Win Take or block every enemy piece.
      • Draw Same position 3 times, or 40 quiet king moves.
      • Hint / Undo make a game unrated.
